## 1. 🎯 Objetivos

- Nombrar habitaciones y partes de una casa u oficina.
- Describir muebles y objetos de cada espacio.
- Usar `there is / there are` para indicar existencia.
- Usar preposiciones de lugar para describir la ubicación de objetos.

---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: THERE IS / THERE ARE

### ¿Por qué se usa?

`There is/are` se usa para decir que algo **existe** o está en un lugar. No describe quién es algo (eso es `to be`), sino que algo **hay** en algún sitio.

---

### Estructura

| Tipo | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | **There is** a sofa in the living room. | **There are** two chairs in the kitchen. |
| Negativa | **There isn't** a dishwasher. | **There aren't** any windows. |
| Pregunta | **Is there** a bathroom upstairs? | **Are there** any shelves? |
| Respuesta | Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. | Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |

**Con `some` y `any`:**

- Afirmativa → `some`: *There are **some** books on the shelf.*
- Negativa → `any`: *There aren't **any** books on the shelf.*
- Pregunta → `any`: *Are there **any** books on the shelf?*

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **move into** | mudarse a | We moved into our new office last week. |
| **move out** | mudarse de | They're moving out next month. |
| **share** a space | compartir un espacio | We share a kitchen with our neighbors. |
| **work from home** | trabajar desde casa | I work from home on Fridays. |
| **set up** | instalar / configurar | Can you help me set up my desk? |
| **tidy up** | ordenar / limpiar | I need to tidy up the office before the client arrives. |
| **on the first/second floor** | en el primer/segundo piso | The meeting room is on the second floor. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Describir un apartamento (anuncio de arriendo)

> **Situación:** Laura publica un anuncio para arrendar su apartamento.

```
FOR RENT — 2-Bedroom Apartment, El Poblado, Medellín

This cozy apartment is on the third floor of a modern building.
There are two bedrooms and one full bathroom.
In the living room, there is a large sofa, a coffee table, and a flat-screen TV.
There are big windows with a balcony view.

The kitchen is fully equipped: there is a refrigerator, a stove, 
a microwave, and a dishwasher. There aren't any pots or pans, 
but there are plenty of cabinets and counter space.

There is a washing machine in the hallway.
There isn't a garage, but there are parking spots available on the street.

Rent: $800,000 COP/month. Available from March 1st.
Contact: Laura Ríos — laura.rios@mail.com
```

**Nota gramatical:** `there is` (singular) y `there are` (plural) alternados según lo que describen. `There isn't / there aren't` para lo que no está disponible.

---

### Caso 2 — Primer día en la oficina nueva

> **Situación:** El manager le muestra la oficina a un empleado nuevo.

```
Manager:  Welcome, Pedro! Let me show you around.
Pedro:    Thank you! It's a nice space.
Manager:  So, there are three floors. Our team is on the second floor.
          There's a reception area and a lobby on the ground floor.
Pedro:    Is there a parking lot?
Manager:  Yes, there is. It's behind the building.
Pedro:    Great. And where's my desk?
Manager:  Over here. There's a desktop computer, a keyboard, and two monitors.
          There aren't any personal items yet, but you can decorate your space.
Pedro:    Is there a printer nearby?
Manager:  Yes, there's one next to the elevator. There's also a break room 
          on this floor — there's a coffee machine, a fridge, and a microwave.
Pedro:    Perfect. Is there a meeting room available today?
Manager:  Room B is free at 3 PM. There are six chairs and a projector in there.
Pedro:    Excellent. I think I'm going to like it here!
```

### Caso 3 — Configurar tu espacio de trabajo remoto (texto instructivo)

> **Situación:** Una empresa publica guías para empleados que trabajan desde casa.

```
Setting Up Your Home Office

Working from home is more productive when your space is well organized.
Here are some tips:

1. Choose a dedicated room or area. Ideally, there should be a desk 
   and a comfortable chair. Avoid working from your bed!

2. Make sure there is good lighting. If possible, there should be a window 
   near your desk. If not, there should be a bright desk lamp.

3. There needs to be a stable internet connection. Check if there's an 
   ethernet cable option — Wi-Fi isn't always reliable for video calls.

4. Is there a whiteboard or a notepad nearby? It helps to write down 
   tasks and keep your focus during the day.

5. There shouldn't be too many distractions. If there are children or 
   pets at home, set clear boundaries for your work hours.

Happy working!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `there should be` — modal para recomendaciones. `there shouldn't be` — negativa modal.

### Caso 4 — Conversación sobre buscar apartamento

> **Situación:** Dos compañeros de RIWI hablan de buscar un lugar para vivir cerca del campus.

Andrés:  I'm looking for a new apartment near RIWI. Do you know any?

## 6. ⏱️ THERE IS/ARE en distintos tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | There is a coffee machine in the break room. |
| **Past Simple** | There was a bookshelf here before the renovation. |
| **Future (will)** | There will be a new meeting room next month. |
| **Future (going to)** | There's going to be a problem if we don't fix this. |
| **Present Perfect** | There have been many changes in the office this year. |
| **Modal (should)** | There should be more natural light in the workspace. |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Confundir `there is` con `it is`

- `There is` → algo existe en un lugar
- `It is` → describe o identifica algo ya mencionado

❌ There is very comfortable. (refiriéndose al sofá ya mencionado)
✅ **It is** very comfortable.

### Singular/Plural

❌ There is three bedrooms.
✅ There **are** three bedrooms.

### `any` vs `some` en preguntas

❌ Are there some chairs available?
✅ Are there **any** chairs available?
