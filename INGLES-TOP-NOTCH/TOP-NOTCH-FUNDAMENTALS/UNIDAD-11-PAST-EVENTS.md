## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Past Simple

### ¿Por qué se usa?

El Past Simple describe acciones o estados **completados** en el pasado, en un momento específico (ayer, el lunes, en 2022). No hay conexión con el presente.

---

### Verbos regulares: añadir -ED

| Regla | Base | Pasado |
|---|---|---|
| Normal → + ed | work | worked |
| Termina en -e → + d | live | lived |
| Consonante-vocal-consonante → doble + ed | stop | stopped |
| Termina en -y (consonante) → -ied | study | studied |

### Estructura del Past Simple

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | S + verb(past) | I **went** to the park. |
| Negativa | S + **didn't** + verb(base) | She **didn't go** to class. |
| Pregunta | **Did** + S + verb(base)? | **Did** you **sleep** well? |
| Respuesta | Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. | — |

> ⚠️ En negativas y preguntas, el verbo vuelve a su **forma base** — `did` lleva la carga del pasado.

❌ Did you **went**?
✅ Did you **go**?

❌ She didn't **worked**.
✅ She didn't **work**.

---

### TO BE en pasado: WAS / WERE

| Pronombre | Was/Were |
|---|---|
| I | **was** |
| You | **were** |
| He / She / It | **was** |
| We / They | **were** |

| Tipo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Afirmativa | It **was** a great weekend. |
| Negativa | They **weren't** at home. |
| Pregunta | **Was** the event interesting? |

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **have** a great/bad time | pasarla bien/mal | We had a great time at the concert. |
| **spend** time | pasar el tiempo | I spent the weekend with my family. |
| **catch up** (with someone) | ponerse al día | I caught up with old friends. |
| **end up** doing | terminar haciendo | We ended up watching Netflix all night. |
| **run into** someone | encontrarse con alguien | I ran into my old teacher downtown. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — El lunes en clase: "How was your weekend?"

> **Situación:** El instructor pregunta a los estudiantes sobre su fin de semana.

```
Instructor: Good morning! How was your weekend?
Carlos:     It was great! I went to a concert on Saturday.
            The band was amazing. I got home really late.
Instructor: Nice! Did you sleep in on Sunday?
Carlos:     Yes! I slept until noon. Then I watched a movie 
            and ordered pizza. Very relaxing.
Instructor: What about you, Valentina?
Valentina:  My weekend was busy. I worked on our React project on Friday night.
            On Saturday, I visited my parents in Barranquilla.
            I came back on Sunday afternoon.
Instructor: Did you take the bus or fly?
Valentina:  I flew. It was only 45 minutes.
Instructor: And the project — did you finish it?
Valentina:  Almost! I wrote most of the code. I just need to fix the CSS.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `How was your weekend?` — `was` pasado de `is`. Mezcla de verbos regulares (`worked`, `visited`) e irregulares (`went`, `slept`, `flew`, `wrote`).

---

### Caso 2 — Cuento de un viaje pasado (texto narrativo)

> **Situación:** Diego escribe sobre su último viaje para su clase de inglés.

```
Last Month's Trip to Cartagena

Last month, I traveled to Cartagena with my family for three days.
We left Medellín on Friday morning and arrived at noon.

We stayed at a hotel near the old city center. The hotel was comfortable 
and the staff was very friendly. In the afternoon, we walked around 
the historic walls. The view was incredible.

On Saturday, we went to the Rosario Islands. We took a boat early in the 
morning and spent the whole day at the beach. I swam in the sea, ate fresh 
fish, and drank coconut water. It was one of the best days of my life.

On Sunday, we visited the Gold Museum and the old colonial churches.
We bought some souvenirs and ate at a traditional restaurant.
The food was delicious — I had sancocho de gallina and patacones.

We flew back on Sunday evening. I was tired but very happy.
I took many photos and I can't wait to go back!
```

**Nota gramatical:** Narración completa en Past Simple. `I can't wait to go back` — presente simple para expresar emoción actual sobre el futuro.

---

### Caso 3 — Descripción de un evento pasado (publicación en LinkedIn)

> **Situación:** Ana escribe sobre un evento de tecnología al que asistió.

```
Last week, I had the opportunity to attend DevFest Medellín 2025. 
It was an incredible experience!

I met over 50 developers from across Colombia and learned a lot 
from the talks. My favorite session was about React Server Components — 
the speaker explained everything clearly and answered all our questions.

I also gave a short lightning talk about TypeScript best practices.
It was my first time presenting at a tech event. I was nervous at first,
but once I started, I felt confident.

The networking session was amazing. I ran into former classmates
and made new connections. We exchanged business cards and talked about 
potential collaborations.

This event reminded me why I love this industry. 
Can't wait for DevFest 2026! 💻🚀

#DevFest #React #TypeScript #Developer #Medellín
```

**Nota gramatical:** `I had the opportunity` — `had` (pasado de `have`). `I was nervous` — `was` para estado. `I felt confident` — `felt` (irregular, pasado de `feel`).

---

### Caso 4 — Reporte de incidente (email formal)

> **Situación:** Un desarrollador reporta un problema técnico que ocurrió ayer.

```
Subject: Incident Report — System Outage — October 4, 2025

Dear Team,

I'm writing to report an incident that occurred yesterday afternoon.

At 2:15 PM, the production server went down unexpectedly.
The homepage didn't load for approximately 45 minutes.
Several users contacted us via email and reported the issue.

I immediately checked the server logs and found a database connection error.
The error started at 2:12 PM after a scheduled update ran automatically.
I contacted the DevOps team and we identified the problem within 20 minutes.

We rolled back the update at 3:00 PM and the system came back online.
All services returned to normal by 3:10 PM.

No data was lost during the incident.
We apologized to affected users and sent email notifications.

Going forward, we will review our update procedures to prevent similar issues.

Best regards,
Pedro Morales
Junior Backend Developer
```

**Nota gramatical:** Reporte formal en Past Simple consistente. `went down`, `didn't load`, `contacted`, `found`, `came back` — mezcla de regulares e irregulares en contexto real.

## 6. ⏱️ Past Simple en contraste con otros tiempos

| Tiempo | Ejemplo | Cuándo |
|---|---|---|
| **Past Simple** | I went to the gym yesterday. | acción específica terminada |
| **Past Continuous** | I was working when she called. | acción en progreso en el pasado |
| **Present Perfect** | I've been to Cartagena twice. | experiencia sin tiempo específico |
| **Used to** | I used to play football every week. | hábito pasado que ya no existe |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Usar `did` + verbo en pasado

❌ Did you **went** to the party?
✅ Did you **go** to the party?

### Usar pasado en negativa

❌ She didn't **worked** yesterday.
✅ She didn't **work** yesterday.

### Pronunciación de -ED

La terminación -ed tiene tres pronunciaciones:
- `/t/` después de consonantes sordas: worked, stopped, liked
- `/d/` después de consonantes sonoras o vocales: lived, played, loved
- `/ɪd/` después de /t/ o /d/: wanted, needed, started
