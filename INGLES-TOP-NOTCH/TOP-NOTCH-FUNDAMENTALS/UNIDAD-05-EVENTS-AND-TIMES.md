## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Preposiciones de tiempo AT / ON / IN

### ¿Por qué son importantes?

En español usamos "a las", "el", "en" de forma bastante libre. En inglés hay reglas fijas según el tipo de referencia temporal. Mezclarlas es uno de los errores más comunes.

---

### La regla

| Preposición | Se usa con | Ejemplos |
|---|---|---|
| **AT** | horas exactas, momentos del día | at 9 AM, at noon, at midnight, at night |
| **ON** | días y fechas específicas | on Monday, on June 14, on my birthday |
| **IN** | meses, años, estaciones, períodos largos | in June, in 2024, in the morning, in summer |

**Truco visual:**
```
IN (grande) → meses, años, siglos
ON (mediano) → días, fechas
AT (pequeño) → horas, momentos puntuales
```

---

### Expresiones de tiempo sin preposición

Algunas expresiones de tiempo **no llevan preposición**:

| Expresión | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| this morning/afternoon/evening | I have class this morning. |
| last night/week/year | I went to the concert last night. |
| next Monday/month | The exam is next Friday. |
| today / tomorrow / yesterday | The event is today. |
| every day/week | She works every weekend. |

## 3. 📚 Vocabulario esencial

### La hora

| Expresión | Significado |
|---|---|
| What time is it? | ¿Qué hora es? |
| It's three o'clock. | Son las tres en punto. |
| It's half past two. | Son las dos y media. |
| It's quarter past four. | Son las cuatro y cuarto. |
| It's quarter to six. | Son las seis menos cuarto. |
| It's 7:45. / It's seven forty-five. | Son las siete cuarenta y cinco. |
| noon / midday | mediodía (12 PM) |
| midnight | medianoche (12 AM) |
| AM | antes del mediodía |
| PM | después del mediodía |

### Días de la semana

Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday · Saturday · Sunday

> 💡 En inglés los días se escriben con mayúscula. "El lunes" = `on Monday`.

### Meses del año

January · February · March · April · May · June · July · August · September · October · November · December

### Fechas

**Cómo leer fechas en inglés:**

| Escrito | Se dice |
|---|---|
| June 14 | June fourteenth |
| March 1 | March first |
| October 31 | October thirty-first |
| December 25 | December twenty-fifth |

> 💡 En EE.UU. el orden es **Month/Day/Year**: June 14, 2025
> En UK y muchos países es **Day/Month/Year**: 14 June 2025

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **make** an appointment | hacer una cita | I need to make an appointment. |
| **cancel** an appointment | cancelar una cita | She cancelled her appointment. |
| **schedule** an event | programar un evento | Let's schedule the meeting. |
| **attend** an event | asistir a un evento | Will you attend the conference? |
| **miss** an event | perderse un evento | I missed the meeting. |
| **on time** | a tiempo / puntual | Please arrive on time. |
| **be late** | llegar tarde | Sorry, I'm late! |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Agenda de clase semanal

> **Situación:** El instructor de RIWI publica el horario de la semana en el grupo.

```
Hi everyone! Here is our schedule for this week:

- Monday: Frontend class at 8:00 AM in Room 3.
- Tuesday: No class in the morning. Code review at 2:00 PM.
- Wednesday: Workshop on TypeScript at 9:00 AM.
- Thursday: Team presentations at 3:30 PM. Don't be late!
- Friday: Free coding time in the morning. 
           Project submission deadline at 5:00 PM.

Important: The exam is on Friday, October 10th, at 10:00 AM.
See you all on Monday!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `at 8:00 AM` — hora exacta. `on Monday` — día específico. `in the morning` — parte del día.

---

### Caso 2 — Reservar una cita por teléfono

> **Situación:** Carlos llama a una clínica para hacer una cita médica.

```
Receptionist: Good morning, City Clinic. How can I help you?
Carlos:       Hi, I'd like to make an appointment with Dr. Ramírez.
Receptionist: Of course. What day works for you?
Carlos:       Is there anything available on Wednesday?
Receptionist: Yes! We have an opening at 10:15 AM or at 3:00 PM.
Carlos:       I'll take the 10:15 AM appointment, please.
Receptionist: Perfect. That's Wednesday, November 6th, at 10:15 AM.
              Can I have your name?
Carlos:       Carlos Herrera.
Receptionist: Great, Mr. Herrera. Please arrive five minutes early.
Carlos:       Of course. Thank you!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `on Wednesday` — día. `at 10:15 AM` — hora. `November 6th` — fecha ordinal.

---

### Caso 3 — Invitación a un evento (mensaje escrito)

> **Situación:** Laura organiza una reunión de cumpleaños y manda invitaciones por mensaje.

```
Hey everyone! 🎉

You're invited to my birthday party!

📅 Date: Saturday, December 21st
🕗 Time: 7:00 PM
📍 Place: La Finca Restaurant, Calle 5 #22-10

Please arrive on time. Dinner starts at 8 PM.
RSVP by Friday, December 19th.

Hope to see you all there!
— Laura
```

**Nota gramatical:** `on Saturday` — día. `at 7:00 PM` — hora. `by Friday` — `by` = antes de / a más tardar el viernes. `in December` — mes.

---

### Caso 4 — Calendario corporativo (email formal)

> **Situación:** Un manager envía los eventos del mes a su equipo.

```
Subject: Team Calendar — October 2025

Dear Team,

Here are the key events for this month:

- October 6 (Monday): Sprint planning at 9:00 AM.
- October 10 (Friday): Mid-sprint review at 2:00 PM.
- October 15 (Wednesday): Company holiday. No office hours.
- October 20–22: Annual conference in Bogotá.
  Please book your travel in advance.
- October 31 (Friday): Sprint demo at 4:00 PM.
                        Monthly report due at 5:00 PM.

Please check your calendars and confirm your attendance
by replying to this email before October 3rd.

Best regards,
Sandra Torres
Project Manager
```

**Nota gramatical:** `October 6 (Monday)` — fecha + día. `at 9:00 AM` — hora. `in advance` — con anticipación. `before October 3rd` — `before` + fecha límite.

## 6. ⏱️ Expresiones de tiempo en distintos tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | The store opens at 9 AM every day. |
| **Past Simple** | The meeting was on Tuesday at 3 PM. |
| **Future (will)** | The exam will be in December. |
| **Future (going to)** | I'm going to attend the workshop on Friday. |
| **Present Perfect** | I have had three meetings this morning. |
| **Present Continuous** | I'm working late tonight — the deadline is tomorrow. |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Usar `in` con días

❌ The class is **in** Monday.
✅ The class is **on** Monday.

### Usar `on` con horas

❌ The meeting is **on** 3 PM.
✅ The meeting is **at** 3 PM.

### Añadir preposición a `last`, `next`, `this`

❌ I went **on** last Monday.
✅ I went last Monday.

### Confundir `in the morning` con `at morning`

❌ I study **at** the morning.
✅ I study **in** the morning.
