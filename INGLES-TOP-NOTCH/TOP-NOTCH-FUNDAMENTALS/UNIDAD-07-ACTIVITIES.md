## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

### ¿Cuándo uso cuál?

Esta es una de las confusiones más comunes. La diferencia clave:

| Tiempo | Para qué sirve | Señales de tiempo |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | rutinas, hábitos, hechos generales | always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day |
| **Present Continuous** | lo que pasa **ahora mismo** o es temporal | now, right now, at the moment, today, this week |

---

### Present Simple — estructura

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | S + verb(+s) | She **works** from home. |
| Negativa | S + don't/doesn't + verb | He **doesn't work** on Sundays. |
| Pregunta | Do/Does + S + verb? | **Do** you **exercise** every day? |

> ⚠️ Tercera persona singular: añade **-s** o **-es**
> - work → works / go → goes / watch → watches / study → studies

---

### Present Continuous — estructura

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | S + am/is/are + verb-ing | I **am studying** right now. |
| Negativa | S + am/is/are + not + verb-ing | She **isn't working** today. |
| Pregunta | Am/Is/Are + S + verb-ing? | **Are** you **watching** TV? |

**Reglas para `-ing`:**

| Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Verbo normal → + ing | work → working |
| Termina en -e → quitar e + ing | write → writing |
| Consonante-vocal-consonante → doblar + ing | run → running / sit → sitting |

---

### Adverbios de frecuencia

Van **antes** del verbo principal, pero **después** de `to be`:

```
I always eat breakfast.       ✅
She is never late.            ✅
He usually studies at night.  ✅
```

| Adverbio | Frecuencia aproximada |
|---|---|
| always | 100% |
| usually | 80–90% |
| often | 60–70% |
| sometimes | 30–50% |
| rarely / seldom | 10–20% |
| never | 0% |

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **do** exercise / homework | hacer ejercicio / tarea | I do homework every night. |
| **make** breakfast/dinner | hacer el desayuno/cena | She makes breakfast at 7 AM. |
| **take** a break / a nap | tomar un descanso / siesta | Let's take a break at noon. |
| **go** running / swimming / shopping | ir a correr / nadar / hacer compras | I go running three times a week. |
| **spend** time | pasar el tiempo | He spends too much time online. |
| **check** email / social media | revisar email / redes | I check my email first thing in the morning. |

> 💡 `do` vs `make` es una fuente clásica de errores. Regla básica:
> - `make` = crear / producir algo (make food, make a decision, make a call)
> - `do` = realizar una actividad/tarea (do homework, do the laundry, do exercise)

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Describir la rutina diaria

> **Situación:** Diego responde preguntas sobre su rutina en una entrevista de inglés para RIWI.

```
Interviewer: Tell me about your daily routine.
Diego:       Sure. I usually wake up at 6 AM. I take a shower and have breakfast 
             by 7. I always have coffee in the morning — I can't function without it!
             I commute to RIWI by bus. It takes about 40 minutes.
             Classes start at 8. I usually have lunch at noon with my classmates.
             In the afternoon, I study or work on projects.
             I go home around 6 PM. I sometimes go running after dinner.
             I go to bed around 11 PM.
Interviewer: Do you always follow this routine?
Diego:       On weekdays, yes. On weekends I sleep in and do different things.
```

**Nota gramatical:** Rutina habitual → Present Simple + adverbios de frecuencia. `On weekdays` / `On weekends` — preposición `on` + día/período.

---

### Caso 2 — Describir lo que está pasando ahora (mensaje de texto)

> **Situación:** Laura le escribe a su amiga explicando por qué no puede hablar.

```
Laura:   Hey! Can you talk?
Friend:  Not right now. I'm studying for the exam.
         My roommate is watching TV really loudly.
         And it's raining outside, so I can't go to the library.
Laura:   Oh no! What are you doing about the noise?
Friend:  I'm listening to music with headphones.
         Also, my mom is calling me on the other line — I'll text you later!
```

**Nota gramatical:** Todas las acciones ocurren en este momento → Present Continuous. `I can't go` — modal de posibilidad negativa.

---

### Caso 3 — Hablar del clima (conversación casual)

> **Situación:** Dos compañeros de oficina hablan del clima antes de una reunión.

```
Carlos:  Wow, it's really hot today!
Ana:     I know. It's 32 degrees and humid. I'm sweating just walking here.
Carlos:  The forecast says it's going to rain this afternoon.
Ana:     Really? I don't have an umbrella.
Carlos:  Me neither. Usually June is sunny in Medellín, but this week 
         it's been crazy. Yesterday it was cold, today it's burning hot.
Ana:     That's Colombia for you — four seasons in one day!
Carlos:  Ha! True. Anyway, is the meeting still at 3?
Ana:     Yes. It starts in 20 minutes.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `It's 32 degrees` — `it` impersonal para el tiempo. `it's going to rain` — predicción con evidencia. `it's been crazy` — Present Perfect para período reciente.

---

### Caso 4 — Rutina de un desarrollador (texto para blog)

> **Situación:** Valentina escribe sobre su rutina como desarrolladora frontend para un blog de tecnología.

```
My Daily Routine as a Frontend Developer

I wake up at 7 AM every day. I never skip breakfast — I always eat 
something before I open my laptop.

I start work at 9 AM. I usually check my emails and Slack messages first.
Then I have a team standup at 9:30 — it's a quick 15-minute meeting
where we talk about what we're doing that day.

Right now, I'm working on a React project with TypeScript. It's challenging
but exciting! I'm also learning more about CSS animations this week.

In the afternoon, I often do code reviews for my teammates.
I rarely work after 6 PM — I believe in work-life balance.
In the evenings, I sometimes read tech articles or watch tutorials.
I go to bed around midnight.

My routine changes during sprints — we sometimes work longer hours,
but it's always worth it when we ship a great product!
```

**Nota gramatical:** Mezcla de Present Simple (rutina) y Present Continuous (lo que está pasando esta semana). Adverbios de frecuencia bien distribuidos.

## 6. ⏱️ Actividades en distintos tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | I exercise every morning. |
| **Present Continuous** | I'm exercising right now. |
| **Past Simple** | I exercised yesterday at 6 AM. |
| **Future (going to)** | I'm going to exercise tomorrow morning. |
| **Present Perfect** | I've exercised three times this week. |
| **Future (will)** | I'll exercise when I finish this project. |

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Usar Continuous con verbos de estado

Ciertos verbos **nunca** van en Continuous: `know, want, need, like, love, hate, understand, believe, have` (posesión).

❌ I am knowing the answer.
✅ I **know** the answer.

❌ She is wanting coffee.
✅ She **wants** coffee.

### Olvidar la -s en tercera persona (Present Simple)

❌ He wake up at 7.
✅ He **wakes** up at 7.

### `Do` vs `Make` en actividades

❌ I make exercise every day.
✅ I **do** exercise every day. / I **work out** every day.
