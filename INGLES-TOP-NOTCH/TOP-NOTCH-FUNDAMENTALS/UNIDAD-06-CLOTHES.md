## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Demostrativos THIS / THAT / THESE / THOSE

### ¿Por qué se usan?

Los demostrativos señalan objetos según su distancia (cercanía vs. lejanía) y número. Son esenciales al comprar ropa porque constantemente señalas cosas.

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| **Cerca** | **this** | **these** |
| **Lejos** | **that** | **those** |

**Ejemplos:**

- *This shirt is too small.* (tengo la camisa en la mano)
- *That jacket on the rack looks great.* (la chaqueta está allá)
- *These jeans don't fit.* (los jeans que tengo puestos)
- *Those shoes in the window are expensive.* (los zapatos del escaparate)

---

### Preguntas con demostrativos

```
How much is this / that?     → ¿Cuánto cuesta esto / eso?
How much are these / those?  → ¿Cuánto cuestan estos / esos?
```

---

### Adjetivos comparativos básicos (para comparar ropa)

| Adjetivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|---|---|---|
| big | bigger | the biggest |
| small | smaller | the smallest |
| long | longer | the longest |
| short | shorter | the shortest |
| expensive | more expensive | the most expensive |
| cheap | cheaper | the cheapest |
| comfortable | more comfortable | the most comfortable |

> Adjetivos cortos (1 sílaba): añade `-er / -est`
> Adjetivos largos (2+ sílabas): usa `more / most`

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Comprar ropa en una tienda

> **Situación:** Ana busca un vestido para una boda.

```
Assistant: Good afternoon! Can I help you?
Ana:       Yes, please. I'm looking for a dress for a wedding.
Assistant: Of course! What size are you?
Ana:       I'm a medium, usually.
Assistant: These dresses just arrived. This blue one is very popular.
Ana:       It's beautiful! Can I try it on?
Assistant: Of course. The fitting room is over there.
--- (after trying it on) ---
Ana:       Hmm, it fits, but it's a bit too long.
Assistant: We have the same dress in shorter styles.
           That green one on the rack is also a medium.
Ana:       I like the green one better. How much is it?
Assistant: It's $85, but it's on sale this week — 20% off.
Ana:       Great! I'll take it. Can I pay with a card?
Assistant: Of course!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `This blue one` / `That green one` — demostrativos + adjetivo + `one` para evitar repetir el sustantivo. `It's a bit too long` — `a bit` suaviza el adjetivo.

---

### Caso 2 — Describir el uniforme / código de vestimenta (email)

> **Situación:** Recursos humanos envía las reglas de vestimenta a nuevos empleados.

```
Subject: Dress Code Policy — New Employees

Dear Team,

Welcome to TechCorp! Here is our dress code policy:

Monday to Thursday:
- Business casual. Dress pants or dark jeans are acceptable.
- Collared shirts or blouses. No T-shirts.
- Closed-toe shoes. No sneakers or sandals.

Friday (Casual Friday):
- Jeans, T-shirts, and sneakers are allowed.
- No shorts, flip-flops, or ripped clothing.

Client meetings:
- Please wear formal attire. Suits and ties for men.
  Dresses or formal blouses for women.

Thank you for your cooperation!
HR Department
```

**Nota gramatical:** `are acceptable / are allowed` — pasiva simple para reglas. `No T-shirts` — forma directa de prohibición en listas.

---

### Caso 3 — Compra online (descripción de producto)

> **Situación:** Carlos ve una chaqueta en una tienda online y la describe a su amigo.

```
Carlos:  Hey, look at this jacket! I want to buy it.
Friend:  Which one?
Carlos:  This black leather jacket. It looks great.
Friend:  How much is it?
Carlos:  $120. But those blue ones next to it are cheaper — only $75.
Friend:  Are those real leather too?
Carlos:  No, they're synthetic. But they look comfortable.
Friend:  I think the black one is better quality. Go for it!
Carlos:  Yeah, you're right. I'll order a large. 
         Wait — it says the large is sold out. 
         I'll have to get the XL.
Friend:  Just return it if it doesn't fit.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `This black leather jacket` — orden: demostrativo + color + material + sustantivo. `those blue ones` — `ones` reemplaza el sustantivo para no repetir.

---

### Caso 4 — Descripción de una persona (texto)

> **Situación:** Sofía describe lo que lleva puesto en una publicación de blog.

```
Today I'm wearing my favorite outfit for a job interview.
I have on a navy blue blouse and black dress pants.
My shoes are black heels — comfortable but professional.
I'm also wearing a small silver watch and a beige coat because it's cold.

For interviews, I never wear jeans or sneakers.
I always try to look clean, neat, and professional.
First impressions matter!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `I have on` / `I'm wearing` — ambas formas son correctas para describir lo que llevas puesto ahora.

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### `wear` vs `put on` vs `take off`

- `wear` → estado: estar usando ropa
- `put on` → acción: ponerse ropa
- `take off` → acción: quitarse ropa

❌ I'm putting a jacket right now. (ya la tengo puesta)
✅ I'm **wearing** a jacket right now.
✅ I'm **putting on** my jacket before going out.

### Olvidar que `jeans`, `pants`, `shorts` son siempre plurales

❌ My jean is blue.
✅ My jeans are blue.
✅ These pants are too tight.

### Usar `this` con plural

❌ This shoes are nice.
✅ **These** shoes are nice.
