## 1. 🎯 Objetivos

- Ordenar comida de forma natural y con opciones específicas.

- Hacer quejas educadas sobre el servicio o la comida.
- Usar `I'd like`, `I'll have`, `Could I...?` en contexto de restaurante.

---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Modales para pedir en restaurantes

### El lenguaje del restaurante

| Situación | Expresión | Registro |
|---|---|---|
| Ordenar | I'll have the chicken, please. | neutral |
| Ordenar (educado) | I'd like the pasta, please. | más educado |
| Pedir algo | Could I have some more water? | educado |
| Preguntar por opciones | What do you recommend? | neutral |
| Pedir sin un ingrediente | Can I get it without onions? | casual |
| Preguntar por ingredientes | Does it come with...? / Is there any...? | neutral |
| Quejarse | Excuse me, there's a problem with my order. | neutral-formal |
| Devolver comida | I'm sorry, but this isn't what I ordered. | cortés |

---

### Estructura de una queja educada

```
Excuse me + [descripción del problema] + [solución esperada]
```

- *Excuse me, the soup is cold. Could you heat it up, please?*
- *I'm sorry, but I ordered the steak medium, and this is well-done.*
- *Excuse me, I think there's been a mistake — I ordered the fish, not the chicken.*

> 💡 Siempre empieza con `Excuse me` o `I'm sorry, but...` — suaviza la queja enormemente.

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **place** an order | hacer un pedido | I'd like to place an order. |
| **make** a reservation | hacer una reserva | I made a reservation for two. |
| **complain** about | quejarse de | She complained about the slow service. |
| **send** food back | devolver comida | I had to send the steak back — it was raw. |
| **leave** a tip | dejar propina | We left a 15% tip. |
| **ask for** the bill | pedir la cuenta | Can we ask for the bill, please? |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Cena de equipo en un restaurante

> **Situación:** El equipo de RIWI celebra el final de un sprint con una cena.

```
Waiter:   Good evening! Welcome to La Terraza. Do you have a reservation?
Carlos:   Yes, a table for five under the name Moreno.
Waiter:   Perfect. Right this way. Here are your menus. 
          Our specials tonight are the grilled sea bass and 
          the lamb stew. Can I start you off with some drinks?
Valentina: I'll have sparkling water, please.
Diego:    Same for me.
Ana:      Could I see your wine list?
Waiter:   Of course. I'll bring it right away.
---
Waiter:   Are you ready to order?
Carlos:   Yes. I'll start with the tomato soup, and for the main 
          course I'd like the grilled chicken. Can I get it 
          without the mushroom sauce?
Waiter:   Of course, sir. And for you?
Valentina: I'm vegetarian — does the pasta come with any meat?
Waiter:   The standard version has prosciutto, but we can make it 
          without. It comes with sun-dried tomatoes and basil.
Valentina: That sounds perfect. I'll have that.
---
[Later]
Diego:    Excuse me — I ordered the fish medium, but this is 
          very well-done. It's quite dry.
Waiter:   I'm so sorry about that. I'll bring you a new one right away.
Diego:    Thank you. I appreciate it.
```

### Caso 2 — Queja por email al restaurante

> **Situación:** Ana escribe una reseña negativa después de una mala experiencia.

```
Subject: Feedback — Dinner on October 4th

Dear Manager,

I visited your restaurant last Saturday with four colleagues to celebrate 
a team milestone. Unfortunately, the experience did not meet our expectations.

First, despite having a reservation, we waited 25 minutes for our table.
Second, two of our orders were incorrect — my colleague received a meat dish 
when she had specifically ordered the vegetarian option.
Third, the service was quite slow — we waited over 40 minutes for our main courses.

That said, the food quality was excellent when it finally arrived, 
and our server was polite when we raised these issues.

We would appreciate a response and hope you will address these 
service issues for future guests.

Thank you for your attention.

Ana Rodríguez
```

## 6. ⏱️ Lenguaje de restaurante en distintos tiempos

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Present Simple | The restaurant opens at 6 PM. |
| Future (will) | I'll have the steak, please. *(decisión espontánea)* |
| Future (going to) | I'm going to try the sea bass tonight. *(plan previo)* |
| Past Simple | We waited 30 minutes for our food. |
| Present Perfect | I've been here three times — always good. |
| Conditional | I would order the lamb, but I'm vegetarian. |

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### `I want` suena brusco en restaurante

❌ I want the chicken.
✅ I'll **have** the chicken. / I'd **like** the chicken.

### `Could I have` vs `Can I have`

Ambas son correctas. `Could` es más suave y educado, preferible en restaurantes formales.

### `The check` vs `the bill`

- `The check` → inglés americano (EE.UU.)
- `The bill` → inglés británico (UK y la mayoría del mundo)
Ambas se entienden universalmente.
