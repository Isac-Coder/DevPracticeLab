## 1. 🎯 Objetivos

- Hacer y responder sugerencias para salir.

- Usar `want to`, `would like to`, `let's`, `why don't we`.

---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Hacer sugerencias

### Estructuras para sugerir

| Estructura | Registro | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **Let's** + verb | informal | Let's go to the concert! |
| **Why don't we** + verb? | informal | Why don't we try that new café? |
| **How about** + noun/gerund? | informal | How about a movie tonight? |
| **What about** + noun/gerund? | informal | What about going out for dinner? |
| **Would you like to** + verb? | educado | Would you like to see a show? |
| **Do you want to** + verb? | informal | Do you want to go dancing? |

### Aceptar una sugerencia

| Expresión | Registro |
|---|---|
| Sounds great! / Sounds good! | informal |
| I'd love to! | entusiasta |
| Sure, why not? | casual |
| That works for me. | neutral |
| I'm in! | muy informal |
| Perfect. Let's do it. | directo |

### Rechazar educadamente

| Expresión | Continúa con... |
|---|---|
| I'm sorry, I can't. | ...I have plans. |
| I'd love to, but... | ...I'm busy that night. |
| Maybe another time? | — |
| I'm not really into... | ...jazz. But I like rock. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Grupo de WhatsApp: planes del viernes

> **Situación:** El grupo de estudiantes de RIWI coordina planes para el viernes.

```
Carlos:   Hey! Anything planned for Friday after class?
Valentina: Not really. Why don't we go out for dinner? 
            I heard there's a great new restaurant on Avenida El Poblado.
Diego:    I'm in! What kind of food is it?
Valentina: Colombian fusion — they mix traditional stuff with modern cooking.
Carlos:   Sounds amazing. How about 8 PM?
Diego:    Works for me. Should we invite the others?
Carlos:   Sure! Let's add Ana and Luis too.
Ana:      Hey! I just saw the messages. I'd love to come!
           But what about going to that jazz concert first?
           It's at 7 at Teatro Metropolitano. We could do both!
Luis:     Jazz? That's not really my thing, honestly.
Ana:      Ha! Fair enough. What about you, Carlos?
Carlos:   I'm into jazz actually. Let's go! We can have dinner after.
Valentina: Okay, I'll come too. Why don't we meet at 6:30 at the theater entrance?
Diego:    Perfect. See you Friday! 🎶🍽️
```

**Nota gramatical:** Múltiples estructuras de sugerencia mezcladas naturalmente. `That's not really my thing` — rechazo educado sin herir. `I'm into jazz` — collocación para gustos.

---

### Caso 2 — Invitación formal por email

> **Situación:** Una empresa invita a sus empleados a un evento cultural.

```
Subject: Team Outing — Friday, November 8

Dear Team,

We would like to invite you to our monthly team outing!

This month, we have two options and we'd love your input:

Option A: Live concert at Parque de los Pies Descalzos (rock/indie music)
          Time: 7:00 PM
          Cost: Free (company-sponsored)

Option B: Film screening at Cine Colombia — Documentary night
          Time: 6:30 PM
          Cost: Free (tickets provided)

Both events are on Friday, November 8.
After either event, we'll go out for dinner together — the company covers drinks!

Would you like to attend? If so, which option do you prefer?
Please reply by Wednesday with your choice.

Hope to see everyone there!

HR Team — TechCorp
```

**Nota gramatical:** `We would like to invite you` — formal. `Would you like to attend?` — invitación educada. `Which option do you prefer?` — pregunta con `which` para opciones limitadas.

### Caso 3 — Conversación sobre gustos musicales

> **Situación:** Dos compañeros descubren que tienen gustos distintos en música.

```
Sofía:  What kind of music do you listen to?
Andrés: I'm really into reggaeton and salsa — I grew up with that.
        But lately I've been listening to a lot of indie pop too.
        What about you?
Sofía:  I love jazz and classical music mostly.
        But I can enjoy almost anything. I'm not into heavy metal, though.
Andrés: Ha, me neither! Have you ever been to a jazz festival?
Sofía:  Yes! I went to the Festival de Jazz de Medellín twice.
        It was incredible. Do you like live music in general?
Andrés: Absolutely. There's something about live music that recordings
        just can't replicate. Why don't we check what concerts are on this month?
Sofía:  Great idea! Let me look it up.
```

## 6. ⏱️ Sugerencias en distintos tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| **Presente (sugerencia)** | Let's go to the concert tonight. |
| **Futuro (going to)** | I'm going to that festival next week — want to come? |
| **Pasado** | Why didn't we go to that concert? We should have! |
| **Condicional** | I would go if I didn't have to work. |
| **Present Perfect** | Have you ever been to a live jazz show? |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### `Let's` no lleva sujeto después

❌ Let's we go.
✅ Let's go.

### `How about` / `What about` + gerundio o sustantivo (no infinitivo)

❌ How about to go?
✅ How about **going**?
✅ How about **a movie**?

### `I'm not into` vs `I don't like`

Ambas son correctas, pero `I'm not into` suena más coloquial y natural en conversación.
