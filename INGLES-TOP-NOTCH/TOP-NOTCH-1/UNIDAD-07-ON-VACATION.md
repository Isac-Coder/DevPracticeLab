## 1. 🎯 Objetivos

- Narrar un viaje o vacaciones en pasado.

- Usar expresiones de secuencia para contar historias.

---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Past Simple + Past Continuous

### ¿Cuándo uso cada uno?

| Tiempo | Uso | Señal |
|---|---|---|
| **Past Simple** | acción completada en el pasado | yesterday, last week, in 2023, at 3 PM |
| **Past Continuous** | acción en progreso en un momento del pasado | while, when, at that moment |

La combinación más común:

```
Past Continuous (acción larga) + when + Past Simple (interrupción)
```

- *I **was swimming** when it **started** to rain.*
- *We **were eating** dinner when the power **went** out.*
- *She **was packing** her bags when he **called**.*

Y también:

```
While + Past Continuous, Past Simple
```
- *While I **was hiking**, I **saw** a monkey.*
- *While they **were exploring** the city, it **started** to rain.*

---

### Past Continuous — estructura

```
Subject + was/were + verb-ing
```

| Tipo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Afirmativa | We **were staying** at a beach hotel. |
| Negativa | They **weren't traveling** by bus. |
| Pregunta | **Was** she **enjoying** the trip? |

---

### Expresiones de secuencia para narrar

| Expresión | Uso |
|---|---|
| First / First of all | primera acción |
| Then / After that | acción siguiente |
| Later | más tarde |
| Meanwhile | mientras tanto |
| Finally / Eventually | al final |
| By the time | para cuando |
| As soon as | tan pronto como |
| Before / After | antes / después |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Contando las vacaciones en clase

> **Situación:** Valentina narra su viaje a San Andrés al volver a RIWI.

```
Teacher:  Welcome back, Valentina! How were your vacations?
Valentina: Amazing! I went to San Andrés for five days.
Teacher:  Oh wow! What did you do there?
Valentina: First, we checked into the hotel and went straight to the beach.
           The water was unbelievable — so clear and warm.
           While we were snorkeling, I saw a sea turtle. 
           I couldn't believe it!
Teacher:  That sounds incredible. Did anything unexpected happen?
Valentina: Yes! On the third day, we were exploring the town market
           when it suddenly started raining really hard.
           We ran into a small café and ended up staying there for 
           two hours. The owner was so friendly — she made us 
           fresh coconut juice and told us about the island's history.
Teacher:  That sounds like a highlight of the trip!
Valentina: It really was. By the time the rain stopped, we had made 
           a new friend. We even exchanged numbers.
           I was taking a nap on the last afternoon when my alarm 
           went off and I realized we had to leave for the airport.
           I almost missed the flight!
Teacher:  Ha! That's always how it goes. Sounds like an unforgettable trip.
Valentina: Absolutely. I'm already planning to go back.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `While we were snorkeling, I saw` — Past Continuous interrumpida por Past Simple. `we were exploring... when it started` — misma estructura. `I was taking a nap when my alarm went off` — interrupción clásica.

### Caso 2 — Blog de viaje (texto narrativo)

> **Situación:** Diego escribe una entrada en su blog sobre su viaje a Bogotá.

```
My Weekend in Bogotá — Three Days I'll Never Forget

I had never been to Bogotá before last month. I was a little nervous 
about the altitude — I'm from Cartagena, so I'm used to sea level — 
but I fell in love with the city almost immediately.

DAY 1
I arrived on Friday evening. While I was checking in at the hotel, 
the receptionist recommended a neighborhood called La Candelaria 
for the next morning. I took her advice.

On Saturday, I woke up early and went to La Candelaria. 
I was walking through the cobblestone streets when I stumbled upon 
the Gold Museum. I had no idea it was there. 
I spent three hours inside — it was breathtaking.

In the afternoon, I took a cable car up to Monserrate. 
The view of the city from the top was overwhelming — 
millions of tiny lights spreading in every direction.

DAY 2
Sunday was for food. First, I went to the Paloquemao market 
and tried every fruit I didn't recognize. 
Then I had ajiaco — a traditional Bogotá soup — at a small 
restaurant near the Bolívar Plaza. 
It was the most comforting meal I'd ever had.

As soon as I finished lunch, it started to rain (classic Bogotá).
I ducked into a bookstore and ended up buying four books.
Eventually, the rain stopped and I walked back to the hotel.

That evening, while I was packing my bags, I was already 
thinking about my next trip. 

Bogotá, I'll be back.
```

**Nota gramatical:** Narración rica con Past Continuous + Past Simple (`while I was walking... I stumbled`, `while I was packing... I was thinking`). Expresiones de secuencia distribuidas naturalmente.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hotel Caribe — Good stay, minor issues

I stayed at Hotel Caribe for four nights in September with my partner.

## 6. ⏱️ Narración de viajes en distintos tiempos

| Tiempo | Uso en narración | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **Past Simple** | hechos principales del relato | We arrived on Friday morning. |
| **Past Continuous** | lo que pasaba de fondo | It was raining when we landed. |
| **Past Perfect** | algo que ocurrió antes de otro evento pasado | I had never been there before. |
| **Present Simple** | hacer el relato más vívido (narración histórica) | So I open the door and — nothing. |
| **Present Perfect** | experiencia hasta el momento | It's the best trip I've ever taken. |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Usar Past Continuous para acciones cortas y completas

❌ I was arrived at 6 PM.
✅ I **arrived** at 6 PM. (acción corta y completa → Past Simple)

### Usar Past Simple para la acción larga de fondo

❌ I swam when I saw the turtle.
✅ I **was swimming** when I **saw** the turtle.

### Orden incorrecto en `when` y `while`

- `When` generalmente introduce la acción corta: *when it **started** to rain*
- `While` introduce la acción larga: *while I **was walking***
