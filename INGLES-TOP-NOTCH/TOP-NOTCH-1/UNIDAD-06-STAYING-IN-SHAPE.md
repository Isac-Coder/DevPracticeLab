## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Gerunds and infinitives after verbs

Después de ciertos verbos, el siguiente verbo puede ser un **gerundio** (-ing) o un **infinitivo** (to + verb). Saber cuál usar es una de las cosas que más separa a un hablante intermedio de uno avanzado.

### Verbos que toman GERUNDIO (-ing)

| Verbo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| enjoy | I enjoy **running** in the morning. |
| avoid | She avoids **eating** late. |
| finish | Did you finish **working out**? |
| keep | Keep **trying**! |
| mind | Do you mind **opening** the window? |
| practice | He practices **swimming** every day. |
| recommend | I recommend **stretching** after exercise. |
| suggest | She suggested **joining** a gym. |
| can't stand | I can't stand **skipping** workouts. |
| miss | I miss **playing** football. |

### Verbos que toman INFINITIVO (to + verb)

| Verbo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| want | I want **to lose** weight. |
| need | You need **to rest**. |
| plan | She plans **to run** a marathon. |
| decide | He decided **to join** a gym. |
| hope | I hope **to finish** the race. |
| would like | I'd like **to get** stronger. |
| learn | I'm learning **to swim**. |
| agree | They agreed **to train** together. |
| try | She tried **to lift** the weight. |
| refuse | He refuses **to quit**. |

### Verbos que toman AMBOS (con diferencia de significado)

| Verbo | Gerundio | Infinitivo |
|---|---|---|
| **stop** | I stopped **eating** sugar. *(dejé de)* | I stopped **to eat**. *(me detuve para comer)* |
| **remember** | I remember **doing** that exercise. *(recuerdo haberlo hecho)* | Remember **to do** your warmup. *(no olvides hacerlo)* |
| **try** | Try **eating** less fat. *(intenta como experimento)* | I tried **to lift** it but couldn't. *(intenté pero no pude)* |

## 4. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales


### Caso 1 — Conversación en el gimnasio

> **Situación:** Carlos conoce a un entrenador personal en el gimnasio.

```
Trainer:  Hey! Are you new here?
Carlos:   Yes, it's my first week. I'm still figuring everything out.
Trainer:  I'm Miguel — I'm a personal trainer here. 
           What are your fitness goals?
Carlos:   I want to lose some weight and get stronger.
           I enjoy running, but I can't stand lifting weights.
Trainer:  That's actually very common! Have you tried doing 
           body weight exercises? They're great for building strength 
           without heavy equipment.
Carlos:   I've tried doing push-ups but I keep giving up after ten.
Trainer:  Ha! Keep practicing — consistency is everything. 
           Do you plan to come every day?
Carlos:   I'd like to come four times a week. I'm trying to avoid 
           skipping workouts like I used to.
Trainer:  Good mindset. I'd recommend starting with 45-minute sessions.
           And remember to warm up before working out and stretch after.
           Many people avoid stretching but it's really important.
Carlos:   Good advice. I always forget to do it.
Trainer:  One more thing — do you mind if I show you the right form 
           for the treadmill? A lot of beginners use it wrong.
Carlos:   Please! I'd love to learn the right technique.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `I enjoy running` — gerundio. `I want to lose` — infinitivo. `I can't stand lifting` — gerundio. `I keep giving up` — gerundio. `I'm trying to avoid skipping` — avoid + gerundio.

### Caso 2 — Plan de bienestar para el equipo (email)

> **Situación:** HR de RIWI presenta un plan de bienestar para los estudiantes.

```
Subject: RIWI Wellness Program — October 2025

Hi everyone!

We're excited to announce our new Wellness Program for October.
We believe that staying healthy helps you learn better and stay focused.

Here's what we're offering this month:

🏃 Morning Running Club — Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 6:30 AM
   We recommend joining even if you're not a runner. 
   Walking counts too! We suggest starting with 20 minutes.

🧘 Yoga Sessions — Tuesdays and Thursdays at 12:30 PM
   Perfect for reducing stress during study hours.
   We recommend stretching regularly — it helps avoid back pain 
   from sitting at a desk all day.

🥗 Healthy Eating Workshop — Saturday, October 12
   Learn how to prepare balanced meals quickly.
   We suggest avoiding processed food during exam weeks.

💻 Digital Detox Tips — Posted on the wellness board
   We recommend taking screen breaks every 45 minutes.
   Try setting a timer to remind yourself.

We hope you enjoy participating! Remember: taking care of your body
helps your mind perform at its best.

The RIWI Wellness Team
```

**Nota gramatical:** `recommend joining`, `suggest starting` — gerundio después de `recommend` y `suggest`. `recommend stretching`, `suggest avoiding` — consistente. `enjoy participating` — gerundio.

## 5. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Usar infinitivo después de `enjoy`

❌ I enjoy to run.
✅ I enjoy **running**.

### Usar gerundio después de `want`

❌ I want going to the gym.
✅ I want **to go** to the gym.

### `stop + gerundio` vs `stop + infinitivo`

- She stopped **eating** sugar. → dejó de comer azúcar
- She stopped **to eat** something. → se detuvo para comer algo (son muy distintos)
