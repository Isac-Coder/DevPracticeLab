---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: HAVE TO / MUST / DON'T HAVE TO / MUSTN'T

### ¿Por qué estos modales?

En el transporte hay muchas reglas, obligaciones y prohibiciones. Estos modales expresan exactamente eso — desde lo que es obligatorio hasta lo que está prohibido.

---

### Estructura

| Modal | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **must** | obligación fuerte (regla o convicción personal) | You **must** show your ticket. |
| **have to** | obligación externa / necesidad | I **have to** catch the 8 AM bus. |
| **don't have to** | no es necesario (pero puedes) | You **don't have to** book in advance. |
| **mustn't** | prohibición (NO debes) | You **mustn't** eat on the metro. |
| **can't** | prohibición (no está permitido) | You **can't** bring large bags on the bus. |

---

### La diferencia clave

| | Significado |
|---|---|
| `don't have to` | No es obligatorio — tienes elección |
| `mustn't` | Está **prohibido** — no lo hagas |

Estas dos se confunden mucho:

- *You **don't have to** buy a ticket in advance.* → (puedes comprarlo el mismo día, está bien)
- *You **mustn't** travel without a ticket.* → (viajar sin tiquete está **prohibido**)

---

### HAVE TO en todos los tiempos

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Present | I **have to** change at Central Station. |
| Past | I **had to** wait 40 minutes for the bus. |
| Future | We **will have to** book the tickets tomorrow. |
| Present Perfect | She **has had to** take a taxi every day this week. |

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **catch** a bus/train/flight | tomar el bus/tren/vuelo | I need to catch the 7:30 train. |
| **miss** a bus/train/flight | perder el bus/tren/vuelo | We missed the last bus. |
| **take** transportation | tomar un medio de transporte | I always take the metro to work. |
| **get on / get off** | subir / bajar | Get off at the third stop. |
| **change** trains | cambiar de tren | You have to change at Central. |
| **book** a ticket | reservar un tiquete | I booked the tickets online. |
| **validate** a ticket | validar un tiquete | Don't forget to validate your ticket. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Preguntando cómo llegar al aeropuerto

> **Situación:** Diego está en Medellín y necesita llegar al aeropuerto Olaya Herrera desde El Poblado.

```
Diego:   Excuse me. I need to get to Olaya Herrera airport.
         What's the best way to get there?
Local:   You have two options. You can take the metro 
         or take a taxi.
Diego:   Which is faster?
Local:   It depends. The metro is cheaper and usually faster,
         but you have to change lines — take Line A to San Antonio,
         then transfer to Line B. Get off at Estadio station.
         From there, you can take a taxi or walk about 10 minutes.
Diego:   How long does the whole journey take?
Local:   About 35 to 40 minutes, depending on the hour.
Diego:   Do I have to buy a ticket in advance?
Local:   No, you don't have to. You can buy it at any metro station.
         But you must validate your ticket before you get on.
         You mustn't jump the turnstile — the fine is expensive.
Diego:   Understood. And what about a taxi directly?
Local:   That's about 20 minutes from here. Maybe 30 in traffic.
         You don't have to worry about transfers, but it'll cost 
         around 25,000 to 30,000 pesos.
Diego:   I'll take the metro then. Thank you so much!
Local:   Good luck! Don't miss your flight!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `you have to change`, `you must validate`, `you mustn't jump` — obligaciones y prohibiciones. `you don't have to buy`, `you don't have to worry` — no es necesario, pero tienes opción.

### Caso 2 — Comprando tiquetes en la taquilla

> **Situación:** Ana compra un tiquete de bus para ir a Cartagena desde Medellín.

```
Ana:     Good morning. I'd like a ticket to Cartagena, please.
Agent:   Of course. When would you like to travel?
Ana:     This Friday, if possible. Morning departure.
Agent:   We have a bus at 7:00 AM and one at 9:30 AM.
Ana:     How long is the journey?
Agent:   About 13 hours with one stop for food.
Ana:     I'll take the 7 AM. One way or return?
Ana:     Just one way, please.
Agent:   Window or aisle seat?
Ana:     Window, please.
Agent:   That'll be $85,000 pesos. Do you have a student discount?
Ana:     Yes, I have my student ID here.
Agent:   With the 10% discount, it's $76,500. 
         You must check in 30 minutes before departure.
         You mustn't bring more than two bags — 
         one carry-on and one for the hold.
Ana:     Got it. Can I pay by card?
Agent:   Of course. 
         Here's your ticket. Seat 14A — window seat. 
         Have a good trip!
Ana:     Thank you!
```

### Caso 3 — Queja sobre retraso de transporte (email)

> **Situación:** Carlos escribe al servicio al cliente de una aerolínea después de un vuelo retrasado.

```
Subject: Complaint — Flight AV304 Delay — October 3rd

Dear Avianca Customer Service,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with my experience 
on flight AV304 from Bogotá to Cartagena on October 3rd.

The flight was scheduled to depart at 10:15 AM but was delayed 
by over three hours, with no clear communication from the staff.
We were told the delay was "operational," but we had to ask 
multiple times for updates — nobody came to inform us voluntarily.

As a result of the delay, I missed a scheduled work meeting at 1:30 PM,
which caused significant professional inconvenience.

According to your stated policy, passengers have the right to 
a meal voucher for delays over two hours. I was never offered one.
I had to buy my own food at the airport.

I would like to request:
1. An explanation for the delay.
2. Compensation for the meal I had to purchase.
3. A refund or credit for the inconvenience.

I look forward to your response.

Sincerely,
Carlos Moreno
Booking reference: AV-304-CMO-0310
```

### Caso 4 — Guía de transporte de una ciudad (texto informativo)

> **Situación:** Un blog de viajes publica una guía de transporte para Medellín.

```
Getting Around Medellín — A Practical Guide

Medellín has one of the best public transport systems in Latin America.
Here's what you need to know before you travel.

THE METRO
The metro has two main lines: Line A (north-south) and Line B (east-west).
You must buy a rechargeable Civica card to use the metro — 
you can't use cash on board.
You don't have to reload much at a time — 5,000 pesos is usually enough 
for a day. You must validate your card every time you enter.
You mustn't eat, drink, or smoke inside the metro stations or trains.

CABLE CARS (Metrocable)
Medellín has several cable car lines that connect hillside neighborhoods 
to the metro system. You have to use the same Civica card.
The views are spectacular — don't miss Line K to Santo Domingo.

BUSES
City buses are affordable but can be confusing for first-time visitors.
You don't have to book in advance — just get on and pay the driver.
However, you must know your route — most buses don't display clear stops.

TAXIS AND RIDESHARE
Taxis are metered. You have to make sure the meter is running 
when you get in. Rideshare apps like InDriver and Uber also work in Medellín.

WALKING AND CYCLING
Many areas in El Poblado and Laureles are walkable.
The city has a free bicycle program called EnCicla — 
you don't have to pay, but you must register online first.
```

## 6. ⏱️ HAVE TO en distintos tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Present | I have to change buses at the terminal. |
| Past | I had to wait 45 minutes — the train was delayed. |
| Future | We will have to book early — it's a holiday weekend. |
| Conditional | If you miss the last metro, you'll have to take a taxi. |
| Present Perfect | She has had to commute by bus all week — her car is broken. |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### `Don't have to` ≠ `mustn't`

❌ You don't have to smoke here. (suena como si fuera opcional)
✅ You **mustn't** smoke here. (está prohibido)

### `Must` no tiene forma de pasado propia

❌ I must go there yesterday.
✅ I **had to** go there yesterday.
> `Must` solo existe en presente. En pasado se usa `had to`.

### `Have to` en tercera persona

❌ She have to take the bus.
✅ She **has to** take the bus.
