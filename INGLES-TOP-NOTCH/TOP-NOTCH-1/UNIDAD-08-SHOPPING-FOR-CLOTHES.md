## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Comparativos y Superlativos

### ¿Por qué se usan?

Cuando compras ropa, constantemente comparas opciones. Los comparativos y superlativos son las herramientas para hacer eso en inglés.

---

### Comparativos — "más... que"

| Tipo de adjetivo | Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 sílaba | + **er than** | cheap → **cheaper than** |
| 1 sílaba terminada en consonante doble | doble consonante + **er** | big → **bigger than** |
| 2 sílabas terminadas en -y | cambia y → **ier than** | easy → **easier than** |
| 2+ sílabas | **more** + adj + **than** | expensive → **more expensive than** |

### Superlativos — "el/la más..."

| Tipo de adjetivo | Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 sílaba | the + adj + **est** | cheap → **the cheapest** |
| 1 sílaba terminada en consonante doble | the + doble + **est** | big → **the biggest** |
| 2 sílabas en -y | the + adj **iest** | easy → **the easiest** |
| 2+ sílabas | **the most** + adj | expensive → **the most expensive** |

### Irregulares (memorizar)

| Adjetivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|---|---|---|
| good | **better** than | **the best** |
| bad | **worse** than | **the worst** |
| far | **farther / further** than | **the farthest / furthest** |

---

### Igualdad y diferencia

| Estructura | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| as + adj + as | igual que | This one is **as expensive as** that one. |
| not as + adj + as | menos que | This isn't **as comfortable as** those. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Comprando ropa para una entrevista

> **Situación:** Ana va de compras para prepararse para su primera entrevista de trabajo.

```
Assistant: Good afternoon! Can I help you with anything?
Ana:       Hi! Yes, I'm looking for something professional.
           I have a job interview next week.
Assistant: Of course! What kind of position is it?
Ana:       It's a tech company — they said the dress code is smart casual.
Assistant: I'd suggest something like this blazer with these trousers.
           They're more formal than jeans but less stiff than a full suit.
Ana:       I like the blazer. Do you have it in a darker color?
           This beige one is nice, but I think navy blue would look better.
Assistant: Yes! We have it in navy, black, and dark gray.
           The navy is probably the most popular for interviews.
Ana:       Can I try the navy one in a medium?
Assistant: Of course. The fitting room is on your right.
--- (after trying it on) ---
Ana:       It fits well, but it's a little tighter than I'd like.
           Is the large available?
Assistant: Let me check. Yes, we have a large in navy.
Ana:       Perfect. This one fits much better. How much is it?
Assistant: The blazer is $180,000 and the trousers are $120,000.
           But if you buy both, you get 15% off.
Ana:       That's a good deal. I'll take both. 
           Does this jacket go with these shoes?
Assistant: Absolutely — black heels are the most versatile option with navy.
Ana:       Great. I'll take everything.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `more formal than`, `less stiff than`, `a little tighter than` — comparativos. `the most popular`, `the most versatile` — superlativos. `much better` — comparativo con intensificador.

### Caso 2 — Devolución en una tienda

> **Situación:** Carlos intenta devolver unos zapatos que compró.

```
Carlos:   Hi. I'd like to return these shoes, please.
Cashier:  Of course. Do you have your receipt?
Carlos:   Yes, here it is. I bought them three days ago.
Cashier:  And what's the reason for the return?
Carlos:   They're too small. I tried them on in the store and 
          they felt fine, but after wearing them for an hour, 
          they started hurting. They're tighter than they looked.
Cashier:  I understand. Would you prefer an exchange or a refund?
Carlos:   I'd prefer an exchange if you have the same model in a larger size.
          These are a 41 — do you have a 42?
Cashier:  Let me check the stock... Yes, we have a 42.
          Do you want to try them on?
Carlos:   Yes, please.
--- (after trying them on) ---
Carlos:   These are much more comfortable. 
          They're not as tight as the others.
Cashier:  Great! I'll process the exchange. 
          Since the price is the same, there's no additional charge.
Carlos:   Perfect. Thank you for your help.
Cashier:  Of course. Enjoy your shoes!
```

### Caso 3 — Comparando compras online vs. en tienda (conversación)

> **Situación:** Dos compañeras hablan sobre sus hábitos de compra de ropa.

```
Sofía:   Do you prefer shopping online or in stores?
Camila:  Definitely online. It's so much faster and cheaper.
         The prices are usually lower than in physical stores.
Sofía:   But don't you worry about the fit?
Camila:  A bit. But most sites have good return policies now.
         It's not as complicated as it used to be.
Sofía:   I always find online shopping more stressful than going to a store.
         I need to try things on — I'm never sure about sizes.
Camila:  That's true. Sizes vary so much between brands. 
         I'm a small in one brand and a medium in another.
Sofía:   Exactly! The most frustrating thing is when something looks
         perfect in the photo and then arrives looking completely different.
Camila:  Ha! The worst is when the color is totally off.
         My worst experience was with a "navy blue" dress 
         that arrived almost purple.
Sofía:   At least you can return it.
Camila:  Most of the time. Some sites are much better than others 
         with returns. I always check the policy before I buy.
```

### Caso 4 — Guía de estilo para entrevistas (artículo)

> **Situación:** Blog de RIWI publica un artículo sobre cómo vestirse para entrevistas.

```
Dress to Impress: What to Wear to a Tech Interview

Getting dressed for a job interview at a tech company can be confusing.
Is the culture casual? Do they care about appearance?
Here's a simple guide.

WHAT TO WEAR
Smart casual is almost always the safest choice for tech interviews.
Think: clean jeans or dress pants, a collared shirt or neat blouse.
Avoid: ripped clothing, overly casual T-shirts, or overly formal suits 
unless the company culture is very traditional.

THE GOLDEN RULES:
1. Cleaner is better than trendier.
2. Well-fitted clothes look more professional than baggy ones.
3. Solid colors are safer than bold patterns in a first impression.
4. Comfortable shoes are just as important as stylish ones — 
   you want to feel confident, not distracted.
5. The most expensive option isn't always the best one.
   A clean, well-fitted outfit from an affordable brand 
   looks better than an expensive suit that doesn't fit.

WHAT TO AVOID
The worst thing you can do is show up looking like you didn't try.
Even in a casual tech environment, making an effort communicates respect.

A tip from our graduates: wear something you've worn before.
Your first interview is the worst time to discover that a new outfit 
is less comfortable than it looked in the store.
```

## 6. ⏱️ Comparativos y superlativos en distintos tiempos

Los comparativos son adjetivos — el tiempo verbal está en el verbo.

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Present Simple | This brand is more expensive than that one. |
| Past Simple | The old store was cheaper than this one. |
| Future (will) | I think the sale prices will be even lower next week. |
| Present Perfect | This is the best deal I've ever seen. |
| Present Continuous | I'm looking for something more comfortable than these. |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Doble comparativo

❌ This is more cheaper.
✅ This is **cheaper**. (no uses `more` con adjetivos cortos)

### Doble superlativo

❌ This is the most cheapest.
✅ This is **the cheapest**.

### Olvidar `than` en comparativos

❌ This is bigger.
✅ This is bigger **than** that one. (cuando hay comparación explícita)

### `Good` → `better`, no `more good`

❌ This is more good.
✅ This is **better**.

## 8. 🧪 Ejercicios

### A — Comparativo o superlativo

1. This jacket is ___ (expensive) than the other one.
2. These are ___ (comfortable) shoes in the store.
3. The blue one is ___ (good) than the black one.
4. That was ___ (bad) shopping experience I've ever had.
5. Online prices are usually ___ (low) than in stores.

### B — Reescribe usando AS...AS

1. The blue dress costs $80. The red dress costs $80.
   → The blue dress is ___
2. The store closes at 8. My friend arrives at 8.
   → My friend arrives ___

### C — Roleplay escrito

Escribe un diálogo de 12+ líneas en una tienda. Incluye: preguntar por una prenda, probársela, comparar con otra opción usando comparativos, y tomar una decisión de compra.

---

## 9. ✅ Respuestas

### A
1. more expensive / 2. the most comfortable / 3. better / 4. the worst / 5. lower

### B
1. The blue dress is as expensive as the red one. / 2. My friend arrives as late as the store closes.
