## 1. 🎯 Objetivos

- Retomar conversaciones con personas ya conocidas.
- Hablar de experiencias recientes y cambios de vida.
- Usar el Present Perfect con `since`, `for`, `already`, `yet`, `just`.
- Distinguir con precisión Present Perfect de Past Simple.

---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Present Perfect — uso avanzado

### Repaso rápido de la estructura

```
Subject + have/has + past participle
```

En TN2 el enfoque cambia: ya no es solo si hiciste algo alguna vez — ahora se usa para hablar de **cambios recientes**, **situaciones que afectan el presente**, y **períodos que no han terminado**.

---

### Los cinco usos clave del Present Perfect

| Uso | Señal | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Experiencia de vida | ever / never | Have you ever lived abroad? |
| Cambio reciente | just | She's just gotten a promotion. |
| Relevancia presente | — | I've lost my keys. (no los encuentro ahora) |
| Duración continua | for / since | He's worked here for 5 years. |
| Antes de lo esperado / todavía no | already / yet | I've already finished. / She hasn't called yet. |

---

### FOR vs. SINCE

| | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **for** | duración (cantidad de tiempo) | for two years, for a month, for ages |
| **since** | punto de inicio | since 2022, since Monday, since I graduated |

> 💡 Truco: `for` + número/cantidad → `since` + fecha/evento

- *I've lived here **for** three years.* → (duración)
- *I've lived here **since** 2021.* → (punto de inicio)

---

### Present Perfect vs. Past Simple — la regla práctica

| Pregunta | Respuesta | Tiempo |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Tiene conexión con el presente? | Sí | Present Perfect |
| ¿Hay un tiempo específico? | Sí (`yesterday`, `last year`) | Past Simple |
| ¿Es una experiencia sin fecha? | Sí | Present Perfect |
| ¿Es una narración de eventos pasados? | Sí | Past Simple |

**El momento en que dices cuándo, cambias a Past Simple:**

- *I've been to Bogotá.* → experiencia general
- *I went to Bogotá **last March**.* → momento específico → Past Simple

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **catch up** with someone | ponerse al día | We should catch up over coffee. |
| **fill someone in** on something | poner al corriente | Fill me in — what did I miss? |
| **keep in touch** | mantenerse en contacto | We've kept in touch since school. |
| **run into** someone | encontrarse con alguien inesperadamente | I ran into my old manager yesterday. |
| **lose touch** with someone | perder el contacto | We lost touch after she moved. |
| **be out of the loop** | estar desactualizado | I've been out of the loop — what happened? |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Reencuentro en un evento de tecnología

> **Situación:** Carlos se encuentra con una excompañera de RIWI en un evento tech en Medellín que no veía desde la graduación.

```
Carlos:   Laura! Oh my god, it's been forever. How have you been?
Laura:    Carlos! Long time no see! I've been great, honestly.
          A lot has changed since we graduated.
Carlos:   Tell me everything. What have you been up to?
Laura:    Well, I've just started a new job at a startup downtown.
          I've been there for about three months now.
Carlos:   That's amazing! What do they do?
Laura:    It's a fintech company. We build payment tools for small businesses.
          I'm working with React and TypeScript — which I obviously 
          learned at RIWI, so that's been super useful.
Carlos:   I knew that would pay off. Have you been in Medellín the whole time?
Laura:    No, actually. I moved to Bogotá for a few months right after graduation,
          but I came back in January. I've been here since then.
          What about you? I heard you got a job at TechCorp.
Carlos:   I did! I've been there for six months. It's been intense but great.
          Have you heard from anyone else from our cohort?
Laura:    I've talked to Valentina a few times. She's already working freelance —
          she hasn't taken a full-time job yet. She says she loves the freedom.
Carlos:   Good for her! We should all get together sometime.
Laura:    Yes! I've been meaning to organize something. Let's do it.
```

**Nota gramatical:** `I've just started` — recently. `I've been there for three months` — continuidad. `I've been here since January` — `since` + punto de inicio. `She hasn't taken...yet` — negativa con `yet`. `She's already working` — `already`.

### Caso 2 — Mensaje de reencuentro en LinkedIn

> **Situación:** Sofía le escribe a un exprofesor que no ha visto en dos años.

```
Hi Professor Martínez,

I hope you're doing well! It's been a while since we last spoke —
I think it's been almost two years since I finished your React course.

A lot has changed since then! I've graduated from RIWI, 
and I've just started my first full-time role as a frontend developer 
at a company called NovaTech in Cartagena.

I've been there for about two months now, and I've already worked 
on my first real client project. It's been challenging but so rewarding.

I've been thinking about your advice about writing clean, 
documented code — I've used it every single day at work.
I haven't forgotten the importance of good component architecture!

I'd love to catch up and tell you more about how the journey has gone.
Have you been teaching at RIWI this year?

Best wishes,
Sofía Navarro
```

**Nota gramatical:** Uso natural de Present Perfect para conectar el pasado (el curso) con el presente (el trabajo). `I've graduated`, `I've just started`, `I've been there for`, `I've already worked`, `I haven't forgotten` — cinco usos distintos en un solo texto.

## 6. ⏱️ Present Perfect en toda su extensión temporal

| Estructura | Ejemplo | Significado |
|---|---|---|
| have + PP | I've started a new job. | hecho reciente con impacto presente |
| have + PP + for | I've worked here for a year. | duración continua |
| have + PP + since | I've lived here since 2023. | desde un punto de inicio |
| have just + PP | She's just graduated. | hace muy poco |
| have already + PP | He's already submitted. | antes de lo esperado |
| haven't + PP + yet | They haven't replied yet. | todavía no (negativa) |
| Have + S + PP + yet? | Have you eaten yet? | todavía no (pregunta) |
| Have + S + ever + PP? | Have you ever been to Tokyo? | experiencia |
| have never + PP | I've never eaten sushi. | sin experiencia |

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Usar Present Perfect con tiempo específico

❌ I've started the job **last Monday**.
✅ I **started** the job last Monday. *(Past Simple — hay fecha)*

### `Since` con duración

❌ I've been here since two years.
✅ I've been here **for** two years. *(for + duración)*
✅ I've been here **since** 2022. *(since + punto de inicio)*

### Omitir `have` en el Present Perfect

❌ She graduated already.
✅ She **has** already graduated.

### Confundir `just` (PP) y `just` (Past)

En inglés americano informal se usa `just` con Past Simple:
- *I just got here.* → (AmE, informal)
- *I've just arrived.* → (más formal, correcto en cualquier variante)
