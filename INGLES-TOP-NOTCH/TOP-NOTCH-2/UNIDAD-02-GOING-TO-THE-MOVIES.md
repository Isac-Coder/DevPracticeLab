## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Verbos de opinión y reporte

### Verbos para expresar opinión sobre entretenimiento

En inglés, para dar una opinión sobre una película no dices solo "is good" — usas verbos que matizan tu postura.

**Verbos + that + cláusula:**

| Verbo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| think | I **think that** the ending was disappointing. |
| believe | I **believe that** the director made a mistake. |
| feel | I **feel that** the story was predictable. |
| find | I **find that** horror movies are overrated. |
| consider | I **consider that** it's one of the best films ever. |
| agree / disagree | I **agree that** the acting was excellent. |

> 💡 `that` es opcional y a menudo se omite en conversación:
> *I think the ending was great.* ✅

**Verbos + infinitivo para describir películas:**

| Verbo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| seem | The plot **seems to** fall apart in the third act. |
| appear | The director **appears to** have run out of ideas. |
| tend | This genre **tends to** be formulaic. |
| claim | The movie **claims to be** based on true events. |
| fail | The sequel **failed to** capture what made the original great. |
| manage | The director **managed to** tell a complex story simply. |

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **go** to the movies / cinema | ir al cine | We went to the movies on Friday. |
| **watch** a film / movie | ver una película | I watched a great documentary last night. |
| **catch** a movie | ver una película (casual) | Want to catch a movie tonight? |
| **rave** about something | hablar maravillas | Everyone is raving about that new Netflix series. |
| **live up to** the hype | estar a la altura del hype | The sequel didn't live up to the hype. |
| **keep someone on the edge of their seat** | mantener en suspenso | The thriller kept me on the edge of my seat. |
| **give something away** | spoilear | Don't give away the ending! |
| **leave an impression** | dejar huella | The film left a lasting impression on me. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Eligiendo una película para ver en grupo

> **Situación:** El equipo de RIWI quiere ver algo juntos el viernes y están debatiendo opciones en el chat.

```
Diego:    Okay, movie night Friday. Suggestions?
Valentina: I think we should watch something fun — 
            no heavy dramas. Something we can just enjoy.
Carlos:   There's a new action movie out. "Eclipse Protocol."
           Everyone seems to be raving about it.
Ana:      Honestly, I feel that action movies tend to be all style 
          and no substance. The plots are usually forgettable.
Carlos:   Fair point. What do you suggest then?
Ana:      There's a really good documentary on Netflix — 
          it's about the origins of the internet. 
          I find that tech documentaries are actually really entertaining.
Diego:    A documentary? On a Friday night? I don't know...
Valentina: What about a thriller? Something that keeps us on the edge 
            of our seats?
Carlos:   I agree that thrillers are more fun to watch together.
           Have you seen "The Conversation"? It's a classic.
Ana:      I haven't seen it yet. Is it old?
Carlos:   It's from the 70s, but it's outstanding. 
           It managed to predict surveillance culture decades before it existed.
Diego:    Okay, I'm in. Old movies can be great.
Valentina: It's settled then. Friday, 7 PM, Carlos's place.
           And Carlos — don't give away the ending this time!
Carlos:   No promises. 😂
```

**Nota gramatical:** `I think we should`, `I feel that`, `I find that`, `I agree that` — verbos de opinión con `that`. `managed to predict`, `tends to be` — verbos + infinitivo. `Everyone seems to be raving` — appear/seem + infinitivo continuo.

### Caso 2 — Reseña escrita para un blog

> **Situación:** Sofía escribe una reseña para el blog de entretenimiento de su universidad.

```
FILM REVIEW: "Everything Everywhere All at Once" (2022)
★★★★★ — A Chaotic, Brilliant Masterpiece

I believe that "Everything Everywhere All at Once" is one of the most 
creative films made in the last decade. And I don't say that lightly.

The plot follows Evelyn Wang, a Chinese-American woman who discovers she 
can access the skills and memories of her alternate-universe selves. 
The concept sounds absurd — and it is — but the directors manage to 
ground it in something deeply human: a mother-daughter relationship 
struggling under the weight of expectation and disappointment.

The acting is outstanding. Michelle Yeoh appears to channel 
every emotion simultaneously, and I consider her performance 
one of the best of the decade. The supporting cast also delivers 
extraordinary work — particularly Ke Huy Quan, who seems to 
rediscover his passion for acting in every scene.

Some critics have argued that the film is too chaotic and overstimulating.
I find that this criticism misses the point. The chaos IS the message.
The film claims to be about nihilism and then dismantles it beautifully.

The cinematography tends to be intentionally overwhelming — it's designed 
to disorient you. The soundtrack, by contrast, anchors every emotional beat.

Does it live up to the hype? Yes. Completely.

You should definitely watch it — but manage your expectations. 
This is not a comfortable film. It's a challenging, unforgettable one.

RATING: 9.5/10
```

**Nota gramatical:** Reseña estructurada con uso rico de verbos de opinión (`I believe`, `I consider`, `I find`), seguidos de `that` o infinitivo (`manage to ground`, `appears to channel`, `seems to rediscover`, `claims to be`, `tends to be`).

### Caso 3 — Debate en clase sobre géneros cinematográficos

> **Situación:** Un instructor de RIWI propone un debate: "¿Las películas de superhéroes son arte o entretenimiento vacío?"

```
Instructor: Let's hear both sides. Carlos, you think superhero movies 
            are legitimate cinema. Defend that.

Carlos:     I believe they're a modern mythology. I think the best ones — 
            like "The Dark Knight" — manage to explore serious themes.
            I find that dismissing the entire genre is intellectually lazy.

Instructor: Strong start. Ana, counterpoint?

Ana:        I agree that some superhero films have merit, but I feel that 
            as an industry, they tend to prioritize franchise building 
            over storytelling. The plots tend to follow the same formula.
            When a film fails to take any real risks, it's hard to call it art.

Instructor: Diego, where do you stand?

Diego:      I think both sides have a point. I find that the problem isn't 
            the genre — it's the volume. When you release 5 films a year, 
            quality tends to suffer. The original Iron Man appears to have 
            been made with genuine passion. Many recent ones don't feel that way.

Valentina:  I'd add that the bar we apply to superhero films seems to be 
            different from the bar we apply to action films generally.
            Nobody claims that action movies are art, and nobody expects them to be.
            Superhero films live up to their genre's standards. 
            Whether that genre should exist is a different question.
```

           The plot appears simple at first but becomes deeply complex.
           You'll find that every scene was planned with precision.
           It's a must-see, no question.

        2. "Nightcrawler" (2014) — American psychological thriller.
           Jake Gyllenhaal's performance is outstanding. 
           The film manages to be deeply uncomfortable and fascinating

           The script is brilliant and the ending will leave you thinking

         I'll try Nightcrawler tonight. Thanks!

## 6. ⏱️ Verbos de opinión en distintos tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Present Simple | I think the acting is excellent. |
| Past Simple | I thought the ending was disappointing. |
| Present Perfect | I've always considered it a masterpiece. |
| Future (will) | I think the sequel will be better. |
| Conditional | I would say it's worth watching, but not twice. |
| Continuous | I'm finding this documentary surprisingly compelling. |

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### `Think` y `believe` en continuo

Estos son verbos de estado — en general no van en continuo cuando expresan opinión:

❌ I am thinking that it's a great film. (opinión estática)
✅ I **think** it's a great film.

> Excepción: `I'm thinking of watching it tonight.` → aquí `think` = considerar una acción, no es opinión.

### `Find` como verbo de opinión vs. de descubrimiento

- *I **find** that documentaries are fascinating.* → opinión
- *I **found** the remote control.* → descubrimiento físico

### Omitir `that` en cláusulas de reporte

Ambas son correctas, pero `that` da más claridad en textos formales:
- *I think it's good.* ✅ (informal)
- *I think **that** it is a significant work.* ✅ (formal/escrito)

## 8. 🧪 Ejercicios

### A — Completa con el verbo correcto

1. I ___ (think/consider) this director to be the best of his generation.
2. The film ___ (manage/tend) to balance humor and drama perfectly.
3. Critics ___ (agree/claim) that the film is based on real events.
4. She ___ (find/feel) that horror movies are too stressful to enjoy.
5. The sequel ___ (fail/appear) to live up to the original.

### B — Transforma las oraciones usando verbos de opinión

1. "The acting was bad." → I feel ___
2. "The plot is predictable." → I find ___
