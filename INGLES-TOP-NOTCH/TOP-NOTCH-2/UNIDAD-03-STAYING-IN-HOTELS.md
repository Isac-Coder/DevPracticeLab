# TOP NOTCH 2 — Unit 03: Staying in Hotels

> **Nivel aproximado:** B1
> **Tema central:** Reservar habitaciones, pedir servicios de hotel y resolver inconvenientes.

---

## 1. 🎯 Objetivos

- Hacer y confirmar reservas de hotel por escrito y por teléfono.
- Pedir servicios y resolver problemas durante la estadía.
- Escribir quejas y solicitudes formales al hotel.
- Usar el pasivo para describir servicios y políticas.

---

## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Voz Pasiva (Passive Voice)

### ¿Por qué se usa?

La voz pasiva es esencial en el lenguaje de hoteles y servicios porque:
1. El foco es lo que se hace, no quién lo hace.
2. Es la voz de los avisos, políticas y confirmaciones formales.
3. Da distancia y profesionalismo a comunicaciones escritas.

---

### Estructura

```
Subject + to be (conjugado) + past participle
```

| Tiempo | Activa | Pasiva |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | They clean the rooms daily. | The rooms **are cleaned** daily. |
| Past Simple | The staff fixed the AC. | The AC **was fixed** by the staff. |
| Future (will) | They will upgrade your room. | Your room **will be upgraded**. |
| Present Perfect | They have confirmed the booking. | The booking **has been confirmed**. |
| Modal | You must book in advance. | The room **must be booked** in advance. |

---

### Cuándo usar pasiva vs. activa

| Situación | Usa | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| El agente es desconocido | Pasiva | My bag was stolen. |
| El agente es obvio/sin importancia | Pasiva | The rooms are cleaned every day. |
| El foco es el objeto/resultado | Pasiva | Breakfast is included in the rate. |
| Comunicación formal/avisos | Pasiva | Guests are requested to return keys by noon. |
| Conversación natural | Activa | The staff helped me immediately. |

## 4. 🔗 Collocations clave

| Collocation | Traducción | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Reserva por teléfono

> **Situación:** Carlos llama a un hotel en Bogotá para hacer una reserva para una conferencia.

```
Receptionist: Good afternoon, Hotel Andino. How can I help you?
Carlos:       Hi, I'd like to make a reservation for two nights, 
              please — arriving Thursday, October 10th.
Receptionist: Of course. How many guests?
Carlos:       Just one. I need a single room, non-smoking if possible.
Receptionist: We have a standard single available. 
              Breakfast is included in the rate.
              The room will be ready by 3 PM on the day of arrival.
Carlos:       Is early check-in available? My conference starts at 2 PM 
              and I'd prefer to drop off my bags before.
Receptionist: Early check-in can be arranged subject to availability.
              There's an additional charge of 80,000 pesos.
Carlos:       That's fine. Is airport transfer offered by the hotel?
Receptionist: Yes, transfers are provided at an extra cost.
              Would you like one to be arranged?
Carlos:       Yes, please. My flight arrives at 10 AM.
Receptionist: Perfect. A car will be sent to Terminal 1 at 10:15 AM.
              You'll receive a confirmation email within the hour.
              May I have your name and email?
Carlos:       Carlos Moreno — c.moreno@mail.com.
Receptionist: Thank you, Mr. Moreno. Is there anything else?
Carlos:       No, that's everything. Thank you!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `Breakfast is included` — pasiva presente. `The room will be ready` — pasiva futuro. `Early check-in can be arranged` — pasiva modal. `Transfers are provided` — pasiva presente. `A car will be sent` — pasiva futuro. `You'll receive a confirmation` — activa para la experiencia del cliente.

### Caso 2 — Email de queja al hotel

> **Situación:** Ana escribe al director del hotel después de una estadía problemática.

```
Subject: Formal Complaint — Reservation #HTL-2025-4421

Dear Hotel Management,

I am writing to formally express my dissatisfaction regarding my 
recent stay at your property from October 5th to October 7th.

Several issues were encountered during my visit:

1. Upon arrival, my reservation could not be located in the system,
   despite having been confirmed by email on September 28th.
   I was kept waiting at reception for 35 minutes.

2. The room I was eventually assigned had not been cleaned properly.
   The bathroom floor had not been mopped, and the towels 
   appeared to have been used. A new room was provided after I complained,
   but no apology was offered.

3. On my second night, I was woken up at 2 AM by noise from a 
   neighboring event. When the front desk was contacted, I was told 
   the noise would be reduced, but this was not done for another 90 minutes.

4. At checkout, I was charged for two room service orders 
   that had not been made by me. The error was corrected 
   only after the itemized bill was requested.

I believe these issues are unacceptable for a four-star property.
I would appreciate a written response and a partial refund 
for the inconvenience caused.

Sincerely,
Ana Rodríguez
```

**Nota gramatical:** `could not be located`, `had been confirmed`, `had not been cleaned`, `had not been mopped`, `was provided`, `was offered`, `was contacted`, `was told`, `was charged`, `was corrected`, `was requested` — uso extenso y natural de la pasiva en registro formal de queja.

---

### Caso 3 — Aviso oficial del hotel (comunicación interna)

> **Situación:** El hotel publica una actualización de políticas en las habitaciones.

```
IMPORTANT GUEST INFORMATION

Dear Valued Guest,

The following information is provided for your comfort and convenience:

CHECK-IN / CHECK-OUT
• Standard check-in time: 3:00 PM
• Standard check-out time: 12:00 PM
• Late check-out can be requested at the front desk. 
  Additional charges may be applied.

HOUSEKEEPING
• Rooms are serviced daily between 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM.
• If a Do Not Disturb sign is displayed, housekeeping will not be performed.
• Extra towels and amenities can be requested at any time.

BREAKFAST
• Breakfast is served in the Garden Restaurant from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.
• Room service breakfast must be ordered by 9:00 PM the previous evening.

NOISE POLICY
• Guests are kindly requested to maintain low noise levels 
  between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM.
• Loud music and gatherings are not permitted in rooms.

SECURITY
• Valuables should be stored in the in-room safe.
• The hotel cannot be held responsible for items left unattended.

We hope your stay is thoroughly enjoyed.
The Management Team
```

**Nota gramatical:** Prácticamente todo el aviso usa pasiva: `is provided`, `can be requested`, `may be applied`, `are serviced`, `will not be performed`, `can be requested`, `is served`, `must be ordered`, `are requested`, `are not permitted`, `should be stored`, `cannot be held`. Esta es la voz natural de las políticas formales en inglés.

### Caso 4 — Conversación en recepción sobre un problema

> **Situación:** Diego reporta un problema con su habitación.

```
Diego:      Excuse me. I need some help with my room.
Receptionist: Of course, sir. What seems to be the problem?
Diego:      The air conditioning isn't working properly.
            The temperature was set to 20°C but the room 
            isn't being cooled at all. It's actually getting warmer.
Receptionist: I apologize for the inconvenience.
              A technician will be sent to your room immediately.
              Have you tried turning the unit off and on again?
Diego:      Yes, I did that twice. It comes on but doesn't cool.
Receptionist: Understood. The unit will be inspected within 15 minutes.
              If the problem can't be fixed tonight, 
              another room will be assigned to you.
              Would you prefer to wait in your room or in the lobby?
Diego:      I'll wait in the lobby — it's cooler here, honestly.
Receptionist: Ha, I understand. Can I offer you a complimentary drink 
              while you wait?
Diego:      That would be great. Thank you.
Receptionist: The technician has already been contacted. 
              I'll come find you as soon as it's been resolved.
```

## 6. ⏱️ Pasiva en todos los tiempos verbales

| Tiempo | Estructura | Ejemplo en contexto de hotel |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | is/are + PP | Rooms are cleaned every day. |
| Past Simple | was/were + PP | The booking was confirmed yesterday. |
| Future (will) | will be + PP | Your luggage will be brought up shortly. |
| Present Continuous | is/are being + PP | Your room is being prepared right now. |
| Past Continuous | was/were being + PP | The lobby was being renovated when we arrived. |
| Present Perfect | has/have been + PP | The error has been corrected. |
| Modal | modal + be + PP | Valuables should be stored in the safe. |

---

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Olvidar el auxiliar `to be`

❌ The room cleaned every day.
✅ The room **is** cleaned every day.

### Confundir pasiva con activa en contextos formales

Ambas pueden ser correctas, pero la pasiva es más apropiada para avisos y políticas:
- Activa: *We will send a technician.* ✅ (conversación)
- Pasiva: *A technician will be sent.* ✅ (comunicación formal)

### `by` para el agente — solo cuando es relevante

❌ The room was cleaned by the housekeeper every day. (el agente es obvio, no añade información)
✅ The room was cleaned every day.
✅ The letter was written **by the manager himself**. (agente relevante)
