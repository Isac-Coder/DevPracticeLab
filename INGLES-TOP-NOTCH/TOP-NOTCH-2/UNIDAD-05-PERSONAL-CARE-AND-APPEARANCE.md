## 2. 🧠 Gramática principal: Get + past participle (causativo)

### ¿Qué es el causativo `get`?

El causativo expresa que **alguien más hace algo por ti** — no lo haces tú mismo. Es la estructura gramatical del cuidado personal porque pagas a alguien para que te corte el pelo, te haga las uñas, te masajee, etc.

```
Subject + get + object + past participle
```

| Ejemplo | Significado |
|---|---|
| I **got** my hair **cut**. | Me cortaron el pelo (alguien lo hizo). |
| She **gets** her nails **done** every Friday. | Se hace las uñas cada viernes. |
| He's **getting** his beard **trimmed** today. | Le van a recortar la barba hoy. |
| I need to **get** my car **washed**. | Necesito que me laven el carro. |

> Compara:
> - *I cut my hair.* → yo mismo me corté el pelo
> - *I got my hair cut.* → alguien me lo cortó

---

### `Have` causativo — alternativa más formal

```
Subject + have + object + past participle
```

- *I had my apartment painted.* → Mandé a pintar mi apartamento.
- *She has her eyebrows done professionally.* → Se hace las cejas profesionalmente.

> `Get` es más informal/conversacional. `Have` es más formal/escrito.

---

### Get causativo en todos los tiempos

| Tiempo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Present Simple | She gets her hair colored every 6 weeks. |
| Past Simple | I got my nails done before the interview. |
| Future (going to) | I'm going to get my hair cut this weekend. |
| Future (will) | I'll get it done tomorrow. |
| Present Perfect | Have you ever gotten a professional massage? |
| Present Continuous | I'm getting my eyebrows shaped right now. |

## 5. 🗣️ Casos de uso reales

### Caso 1 — Haciendo una cita en el salón

> **Situación:** Ana llama a su salón para hacer una cita antes de una presentación importante en RIWI.

```
Receptionist: Good morning, Estilo Único Salon. How can I help you?
Ana:          Hi! I'd like to make an appointment, please.
              I need to get my hair cut and colored.
Receptionist: Of course. What day works for you?
Ana:          This Saturday if possible. I have a big presentation 
              on Monday and I want to look polished.
Receptionist: We have an opening at 10 AM or 2 PM on Saturday.
Ana:          I'll take the 10 AM. How long will it take?
Receptionist: Getting a cut and color done usually takes 
              about two to three hours depending on your hair length.
Ana:          My hair is medium length. I also want to get 
              my eyebrows shaped — is that possible in the same appointment?
Receptionist: Absolutely. We can have everything done in one session.
              Which stylist would you like?
Ana:          I usually get my hair done by Valentina, if she's available.
Receptionist: Let me check... Yes, Valentina is free at 10 AM.
Ana:          Perfect. And I'd like to get balayage done — 
              I've been wanting to get my color updated for months.
Receptionist: Great choice. I'll put you down for a cut, 
              balayage, and eyebrow shaping with Valentina 
              on Saturday at 10 AM. Your name?
Ana:          Ana Rodríguez.
Receptionist: Perfect, Ana. We'll see you Saturday! 
              Don't forget to arrive with clean, dry hair.
Ana:          Got it. Thank you!
```

**Nota gramatical:** `get my hair cut and colored`, `get my eyebrows shaped`, `get my hair done`, `get my color updated`, `have everything done` — causativo `get/have` repetido naturalmente en contexto de salón.

### Caso 3 — En el salón: describiendo lo que quieres

> **Situación:** Diego va al barbero por primera vez a un nuevo lugar y describe exactamente lo que quiere.

```
Barber:  Hey! What are we doing today?
Diego:   Hi. I want to get my hair cut shorter on the sides 
         and keep some length on top. Not too short though.
Barber:  Like a fade on the sides?
Diego:   Yeah, a mid fade. And I want to get my beard trimmed too —
         just clean up the edges and take it down a bit.
Barber:  Sure. Any specific length on top?
Diego:   Maybe 4-5 cm? I don't want to lose too much.
         I usually get it cut every 5 or 6 weeks.
Barber:  Good. Do you want the top styled or just cut?
Diego:   Just cut for now. I'll style it myself.
Barber:  And the beard — do you want me to use a guard or scissors?
Diego:   Guard, please. A number 3 on the beard.
Barber:  Perfect. Sit back and relax.
--- (after the cut) ---
Barber:  What do you think?
Diego:   I love it. How much do I owe you?
Barber:  35,000 for the cut and beard.
Diego:   That's great. Here you go, keep the change.

         just a subtle effect on the top.
```

## 7. ⚠️ Errores frecuentes

### Confundir causativo con pasiva

- **Causativa**: *I got my hair cut.* → yo lo pedí, alguien lo hizo
- **Pasiva**: *My hair was cut.* → enfatiza el hecho, no la causa

### Omitir el participio pasado

❌ I got my hair.
✅ I got my hair **cut**. (el participio pasado es obligatorio)

### Usar el infinitivo en vez del participio

❌ I got my nails to paint.
✅ I got my nails **painted**.
